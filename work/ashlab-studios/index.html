Frequently asked questions

Where can I download an app or find an update?

Open the relevant app page and follow its download or release links. Each page shows the distribution channel for that app.

Which terms and privacy policy apply?

The AshLab Studios Terms of Use apply across the studio. Look on your app’s page for its privacy policy and any additional app terms.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include the app version, device model, Android version and the steps that reproduce the issue. Remove personal information from screenshots and keep exports, account identifiers and backups out of public reports.

How do I delete an app’s data?

Open the app’s privacy policy for its deletion steps. It explains what to do with saved backups or accounts where those features are available.

Support

For troubleshooting and public bug reports, start on the app’s project page. For privacy requests, complaints or anything involving personal information, message Ashot Grigorian privately on LinkedIn. Include the app name and version.
