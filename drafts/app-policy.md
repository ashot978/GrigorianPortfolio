# App privacy policy and terms template

Complete this draft before publishing any app policies.

## Privacy policy

Publish an HTML page for each app at `apps/<app>/privacy.html`. Cover these points based on how the app actually works.

- App and developer names, privacy contact and effective date.
- Data the app reads, stores locally or sends, including data handled by SDKs for features such as crash reporting, analytics or advertising.
- Reasons for processing, recipients, security measures and retention periods.
- How users can submit privacy and deletion requests. If users can create an account, describe how to request account deletion from both the app and a public website.
- Check that the privacy policy, the app and the Play Console Data safety declaration describe the same practices.

## Terms of use

Include the provider’s details, the apps covered, the effective date, rules of use and a support contact. Add provisions for accounts, payments, cancellation, refunds or user content where relevant.
