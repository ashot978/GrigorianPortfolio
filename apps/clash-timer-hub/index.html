Frequently asked questions

Where can I download Clash Timer Hub?

The Android preview builds and release notes are on GitHub Releases. Save a backup if you want to keep your timers and settings before reinstalling or resetting the app.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold an empty area on your Android home screen, open the Widgets menu and choose Clash Timer Hub. Resize the widget to show more accounts.

How do I import my timers?

In Clash of Clans, open Settings → Data Export → Copy. Use the floating Sync button or open Clash Timer Hub’s settings and choose Sync from clipboard.

Why are my timers or notifications out of date?

Import a fresh Clash export to refresh your timers. If notifications still arrive late, check Android’s notification and exact alarm permissions and the device’s battery settings. You can test notifications and alarms from the app’s settings.

What happens to my timers if I uninstall or reset the app?

Clearing app storage removes local data. Export a backup first if you want to restore it later. See the app’s privacy policy for how to remove local data and backups separately.
