Clash Timer Hub privacy policy

Updated 18 November 2026 · Android build #568

Ashot Grigorian develops Clash Timer Hub under the name AshLab Studios. This policy covers the Android app. The website privacy notice covers the project website and browser demo.

Clash data and settings

When you import an export from Clash of Clans, the app reads the content you share or copy to the clipboard. It saves player tags, account aliases, upgrade timers, helpers, equipment and collection data needed for the dashboard, widgets and notifications. App preferences, such as language, widgets and notification settings, are also stored. The app keeps this information in private app storage on your device and does not require an AshLab Studios account.

Backups and Google Drive

When you export a backup, it can contain player tags, account names, timers, collections and app settings. If you copy it to your clipboard or share it as a file, the clipboard and the app or service you choose may retain a copy.

If you connect Google Drive, Google handles authorisation and provides an access token for the app’s backup requests. Clash Timer Hub uses file-level access to find, create, update or restore backups it has access to. Google receives the contents of the backup when you save it to Drive over HTTPS, as explained in Google’s privacy policy. If you enable automatic backup, the app can update that backup after a sync. You can turn this off or disconnect Drive in the app. You can also remove access in your Google Account settings. Android may include app storage in system backup or device transfers, depending on your device settings.

Updates and text recognition

Available releases are checked through GitHub by default. GitHub or another configured update provider receives network information such as your IP address when your device connects. Update requests do not include Clash exports. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Where a text recognition feature is used, Google ML Kit processes images and recognised text on the device. Google may receive SDK performance and usage metrics as described in its ML Kit privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Imported data and preferences remain in the app until changed or removed. Clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its on-device data. Delete exported backup files separately from Drive or any other location where you saved them. Disconnecting Drive stops the app’s access and leaves existing files in your Drive. Manage Android’s own backup separately in your device or Google Account settings.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact the developer, your message and any details you choose to share may be used to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with the request and any follow-up or legal obligation. Use the private contact route for requests about your data. Keep player tags, exports and backups out of public GitHub issues.

Where applicable, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data or exercise other privacy rights. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, such as the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
